Hawthorn & Hawthorn East

Kew & Kew East

Quiet streets around Victoria Park, Hays Paddock and Willsmere Park, plus the Outer Circle Linear Park trail.

Gentle routes around Beckett Park, Mont Albert Park and the King Street Chain reserves.

05 Questions, answered

Good questions.
straight answers.

How much does a dog walk cost in Boroondara?

A 30-minute walk is $50 AUD and a 1-hour walk is $80 AUD, per dog. An additional pet on the same walk is $10 AUD each.

Which suburbs do you cover?

We walk in the City of Boroondara and nearby: Hawthorn, Hawthorn East, Kew, Kew East, Camberwell, Canterbury, Surrey Hills, Balwyn, Mont Albert, Glen Iris, and the streets around them.

Do you walk more than one dog at a time?

Yes. If you have more than one dog, they can join the same walk for an extra $10 AUD each.

How do I book a walk?

Pick a walk and a time in the booking calendar and pay online. We'll confirm your walk. If we can't take it, you'll get a full refund. Prefer to chat first? Message us on Instagram or use the contact form.
