05 Questions, answered

Good questions.
straight answers.

How much does a dog walk cost in Boroondara?

A 30-minute walk is $50 AUD and a 1-hour walk is $80 AUD, per dog. An additional pet on the same walk is $10 AUD each.

Which suburbs do you cover?

We walk in the City of Boroondara and nearby — Hawthorn, Kew, Camberwell, Canterbury, Surrey Hills, Balwyn, Glen Iris, Hawthorn East, Mont Albert, and the streets around them.

Do you walk more than one dog at a time?

Every walk is one on one. If you have more than one dog, they can join the same walk for an extra $10 AUD each — still one walker, still focused on your pets.

How do I book a walk?

Pick a 30-minute or 1-hour walk and a time in the booking calendar on this page. Bookings are requests: we'll confirm, then send an invoice. Prefer to chat first? Message us on Instagram or use the contact form.
